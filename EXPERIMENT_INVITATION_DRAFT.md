# Draft invitation (NOT SENT - for review before any contact)

Status: draft only. No contact has been made. Channel and recipients are deliberately unresolved.

## Purpose of this message

Ask a transport operator whether a prepared preview reduces the work of finding opportunities
they can actually pursue. It is not a sales message and must not become one: no pricing, no
promises, no claim that the data is complete when it is not.

## Candidate message (short form)

    Subject: Six East Sussex school transport runs closing this week - is a list like this useful?

    Hello,

    I have put together a short list of school and passenger transport runs that East Sussex
    County Council published recently, with the closing time for each and a note of what the
    notice does not say (contract value, insurance and DBS requirements, lot structure).

    Example entry: Taxi and MPV, 6 seat MPV, passenger assistant required, harness and space
    needed, route TN39 5AR to Torfield School, closes 17 September 10:00, bid via SProc.net
    under a restricted procedure.

    I am trying to find out whether a list like this saves any real time compared with the
    alerts you already get. If it does, I would like to keep sending it. If it does not, I
    would rather know that now.

    Two questions, if you have a minute:
    1. How many of those could you actually take on with your current vehicles?
    2. What is missing that you would need before bidding on one?

    I am not selling anything at this stage and there is no charge. Reply "no thanks" and
    I will not contact you again.

    [Name], [contact], [date]

## Notes for whoever sends it

- Must comply with UK business-to-business direct marketing rules: identify the sender
  clearly and provide a working opt-out (the "no thanks" line above).
- Must not state or imply that the buyer will invite the operator to bid. The notices are
  restricted-procedure notices: registration does not mean invitation.
- Must not promise completeness, accuracy beyond the timestamp, or continued delivery.
- Recipients unresolved. Options remain: (a) one operator, read together in person or by
  phone - cheapest and highest signal; (b) direct email to a small number of named operators
  using publicly published business addresses; (c) a post in an operator community - lowest
  signal, highest risk of reading as promotion.
